| 0035 | 2026-11-19 | Where the model runs is decided once per worker by `machine.detect()` (ctypes only: nvcuda + Windows topology, never torch/onnxruntime, never in the long-running server - a short `python -m parakeet_stt machine` child tells it): onnxruntime without its CUDA provider DLL, no NVIDIA driver, cuInit 100 / no device (also a hidden card), compute capability < 5.2 or < 5.5 GiB → the processor with that reason; any other driver answer → the card, so its real error shows (never a silent slowdown). | 0034 | 2026-11-19 | A full GPU memory is its own outcome, not a failure: onnxruntime's allocation errors (arena, CUDA, cuBLAS/cuDNN) and a model part that came up on the CPU while little VRAM is free (the driver's figure) raise `EngineOutOfMemory`; every session's CPU fallback is turned off and checked after each slice; the worker answers `gpu_memory`, the runner keeps the checkpoint, sends the job back uncounted and pauses the whole queue for 120 s (`Status.paused_kind` / `PausedEvent.kind` = "gpu_busy" / "gpu_memory", the window's own text); the 3rd time in a row the job fails readably and the queue goes on. | 0033 | 2026-11-19 | A running job keeps its progress in a sealed checkpoint (`checkpoint.py`, role CHECKPOINT): the speech windows and the words of every whole slice of 32 windows, saved at most every 5 s of work and once more when the run is stopped (closing, cancel, full memory). It is used again only when the app version, a digest of every Python file, the engine (model revision, provider, options, library versions), the speech detector (model revision, options) and the sha256 of the decoded samples are all equal and its windows hold together; otherwise the job starts over. Recognition always runs in slices (CLI and app alike). A worker that dies on its own is now run again from its checkpoint (counted; failed after 2 starts) - this replaces ADR 0013's "a job interrupted by a crash restarts from the start". Kept on close, GPU busy, GPU memory, a crash under the limit and an interrupted start; deleted on done, cancel (also of a waiting job), error, the last failure and remove; a folder holding only a checkpoint is swept | A resumed run must give the very same subtitles: measured identical (words, times, confidences) on 3 h 33 min and 5 h 26 min lectures through the app after a killed worker, a closed app and a memory error; a whole 3 h 33 min run 121 s, the rest after stopping at half 47 s; one save of a 5 h lecture's progress 56 ms.
