Third-party software in TalkSubs
================================

TalkSubs itself is under the MIT licence (LICENSE). The app window (parakeet_stt/server/static) is built from
the libraries below; their notices follow. The Python libraries in the download package carry their own licence
files inside python/Lib/site-packages (*.dist-info); FFmpeg's notice and licences are in the ffmpeg folder.
The speech models (NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 and v3, CC BY 4.0) and the voice detector (Silero VAD, MIT) are
credited in README.md and on the app's Help page.
